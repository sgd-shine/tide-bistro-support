潮汐小馆：合成与故事 · 技术支持

适用版本：免费版 1.3.0 起。第一季有 8 个剧情日、38 段对话；“剧情日”是章节，不要求连续 8 个自然日游玩。打完第一季后，第二季「灯塔下的店」每周五解锁一章，等新章的日子里还能交订单、补食谱、继续翻新和参加潮汐季活动。

游戏从哪里开始？体力用完怎么办？

点案板上的生成器取得食材，把相同食材拖到一起合成，备齐顾客需要的物品后交付订单。待办本会提示接下来要做的事；达到相应等级后，每天还有三个小目标。

体力会随时间恢复；升级、每日礼盒、体力瓶和游玩奖励也能补充。小铺提供自愿购买的体力和贝壳币，不购买也能完成主线；游戏没有广告或订阅。

已购买的东西在哪里？怎样恢复会员？

购买由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。打开游戏“设置 → 恢复购买”可重新核对一次性小馆会员。早期买过付费下载版的玩家，在符合 Apple 购买记录的情况下会自动获得会员。消耗型贝壳币、体力和开张礼包一旦使用，不能通过“恢复购买”重新发放；跨设备存档会同步假名化的交易指纹以避免同一笔商品重复到账，原始交易号仍留在购买设备本机。如商品已扣费但未到账，请先保留 App 与存档，稍后重新打开游戏并联系支持，提供购买时间和 Apple 的订单参考信息，不要发送 Apple 账号密码或完整付款资料。

退款请求请按 Apple 的退款说明办理；退款或撤销可能影响相应购买权益。

换 iPhone 后如何继续进度？

当前设备会自动保存进度。正式发布版在可用 iCloud 账号下会自动同步进度，无须在游戏中打开同步开关。请在新旧设备使用同一 Apple 账号，并确认系统允许本 App 使用 iCloud；等待新设备显示正确进度后，再考虑清理旧设备。网络或 iCloud 暂时不可用时，仍可在当前设备玩主线，跨设备同步可能延后。

如果两台设备的进度不同、同步一直未完成，或你不确定哪个存档是最新的，请先保留两台设备上的 App，不要重置、删除或反复覆盖存档；联系支持并说明两台设备的版本和大致进度。重置游戏会开始新进度，之后可能同步到同一账号下的其他设备。删除 App 不会直接清除已同步到 iCloud 的存档。

好友周榜怎么用？不登录能玩吗？

点案板页的奖杯进入好友周榜：5 级以前在顶栏，5 级以后挪进活动条。自愿登录 Game Center 后，你可以与好友比较本周接待的顾客数量；一周结束后在周榜页领取对应的游戏内奖励。第一期从 2026-10-05 00:00 UTC 开始。若榜单暂不可用，请检查 iPhone“设置 → Game Center”的登录状态及网络，再回到游戏重试。不登录也能照常玩主线。

第二季的新章什么时候开？潮汐季是什么？

打完第一季后，第二季每周五（按手机本地日期，早上 4 点切日）解锁一章；上一章打完才能进下一章，等新章的日子回到“小馆日常”，待办本会写着下一章哪天开。潮汐季每 4 周一季：收藏册、30 级奖励全免费的季票、周末集市和每周轮换的装饰阶梯 / 订单冲刺，5 级以后从案板页的活动条进入。活动日历写在游戏里，不需要联网。

为什么没有体力、生成器、新章或集市提醒？

本地提醒需要在游戏内选择开启，并允许 iOS 通知权限。游戏会在第一次体力用完或追平剧情时问一次是否需要提醒，之后可在“设置”里随时开关。你可在 iPhone“设置 → 通知 → 潮汐小馆”查看或关闭授权。提醒只作提示；能否收到取决于设备和系统设置，不影响体力或生成器按游戏规则恢复。

怎样重看教程或从头开始？

游戏“设置 → 重看教程”不会清除进度。“设置 → 重置游戏”会重新开始，操作前请看清确认提示；使用 iCloud 时，重置可能影响同一账号下的其他设备。遇到存档异常时不要先删除 App。

Tide Bistro: Seaside Merge — Support

Applies to: free edition, version 1.3.0 and later. Season 1 has 8 story days and 38 scenes. Story days are chapters, not a requirement to play on eight consecutive calendar days. After Season 1, Season 2, “The Shop by the Lighthouse,” unlocks one chapter every Friday; between chapters you can keep serving orders, filling the recipe book, renovating, and playing Tide Season events.

How do I play? What if I run out of energy?

Tap a generator for ingredients, drag matching items together, and deliver a customer's completed order. Grandma's notebook shows what comes next. After you reach the required level, three daily goals offer small rewards.

Energy refills over time. Level-ups, daily gifts, energy bottles, and play rewards also help. The shop offers optional energy and shells; you can finish the main story without buying anything. There are no ads or subscriptions.

Where is my purchase? How do I restore membership?

Apple's App Store handles purchases. Use “Settings → Restore Purchases” in the game to check your one-time Bistro Membership again. Eligible players who bought the earlier paid edition receive membership automatically based on Apple's purchase record. Used consumable shells, energy, and the Opening Day Pack are not redelivered by Restore Purchases. A synced save carries pseudonymous transaction fingerprints to prevent the same item from being delivered twice across devices; the raw transaction identifier stays on the purchasing device. If you were charged but nothing appeared, keep the app and save, reopen the game later, and contact support with the purchase time and an Apple order reference. Never send an Apple Account password or complete payment details.

For refunds, follow Apple's refund instructions. A refund or revocation may change the related entitlement.

How do I continue on a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on the current device. In the release build, it syncs automatically when an iCloud account is available; there is no in-game sync switch. Use the same Apple Account on both devices and make sure the system allows this app to use iCloud. Wait until the correct progress appears on the new device before clearing the old one. You can keep playing the main story on the current device during a network or iCloud interruption; cross-device sync may happen later.

If the devices show different progress, sync does not finish, or you are unsure which save is newest, keep the app on both devices. Do not reset, delete, or repeatedly overwrite saves. Contact support with both app versions and a short description of the progress on each device. Resetting the game starts new progress, which may then sync to your other devices using the same account. Deleting the app does not directly erase a save already synchronized to iCloud.

How does the friends leaderboard work?

Tap the trophy on the board screen: it sits above the board until level 5, then moves into the event strip. Game Center sign-in is optional. With it, you can compare this week's customers served with your friends and claim an in-game reward after the week ends. The first period starts on October 5, 2026 at 00:00 UTC. If the board is unavailable, check iPhone Settings → Game Center and your network, then try again. The main story works without Game Center.

When do new Season 2 chapters arrive? What is Tide Season?

After Season 1, a new chapter unlocks every Friday by your phone's local date (the game day starts at 4 a.m.). Each chapter opens after you finish the previous one; while you wait, the notebook shows when the next chapter arrives. Tide Season runs in four-week seasons: an album, a 30-tier pass with every reward free, the Weekend Market, and a weekly event that alternates between the Decor Ladder and the Order Sprint. From level 5, open it from the event strip on the board screen. The event calendar is built into the game and works offline.

Why am I not receiving energy, generator, chapter, or market reminders?

Local reminders require you to turn them on in the game and allow iOS notification permission. The game asks once—the first time you run out of energy or catch up with the story—and you can switch reminders on or off in Settings at any time. You can review or disable permission in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tide Bistro. Reminders are informational; delivery depends on device and system settings and does not change the game's refill rules.

Can I replay the tutorial or restart?

“Settings → Replay Tutorial” keeps your progress. “Settings → Reset Game” starts over; read the confirmation carefully. If you use iCloud, a reset may affect other devices using the same account. If a save looks damaged, do not delete the app first.
